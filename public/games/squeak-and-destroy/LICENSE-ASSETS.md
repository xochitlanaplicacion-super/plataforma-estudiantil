# Asset licence

The [MIT licence](LICENSE) covers the **source code** only — `js/`, `css/`,
`*.php`, `*.html` and the build tooling. The **assets** are licensed separately,
in two groups.

## 1. Original assets by Los Polos Amigos Kft.

Everything we made ourselves — the logo, app icon, intro logo, the hero
screenshot and every code-drawn graphic (animal bodies, class overlays, terrain
painting, particles, HUD) — is licensed:

> **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International (CC BY-NC 4.0)**
> <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/>

You may remix, adapt and redistribute them for **non-commercial** purposes with
attribution to *Los Polos Amigos Kft. — Squeak & Destroy*. For a commercial
licence, open an issue.

Note that the *code* that draws those graphics is MIT — if you fork the game and
replace the art, nothing here constrains you.

### AI disclosure

`assets/logo.png`, `assets/logo-intro.png`, `assets/appicon.png` and
`assets/icon-192.png` were produced with AI image tools and then hand-curated.
We hold the commercial rights via those tools' terms. Storefronts that require
an AI-content disclosure (e.g. Steam) should be told about these files.

## 2. Third-party assets

Fonts, weapon icons, music and sound effects come from other authors under their
own licences (OFL, CC0, CC BY). Those licences govern — this file does not
override them. Every one is listed in [ATTRIBUTIONS.md](ATTRIBUTIONS.md), and the
CC BY ones **must** stay credited if you redistribute the game.

## 3. What is deliberately missing

Some art the game shipped with is under licences that permit *use* but forbid
*redistribution*, so it is not in this repository. The game runs without it —
it falls back to code-drawn graphics. See
[docs/ASSET_LICENSING.md](docs/ASSET_LICENSING.md).
